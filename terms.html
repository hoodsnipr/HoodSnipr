1. What HoodSnipr is

HoodSnipr is a non-custodial interface for discovering and trading tokens on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663). It displays market data and constructs transactions that your wallet submits to public Uniswap contracts. We never take possession of your assets and cannot move, freeze, reverse, or recover them.

3. Fees

  • The fee is charged before the swap executes and is non-refundable once signed.
  • If a swap fails after a fee has been paid, the amount is credited against your next trade automatically. That credit is stored in your browser and is lost if you clear site data.
  • Network gas is paid to the chain, never to us, and is never refundable.
